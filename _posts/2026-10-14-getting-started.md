How do you get started with AI?

At the moment AI feels, paradoxically, super simple and overwhelmingly complex.

Super simple: it's just a "prompt", you feed it something to do and it does it, right?

Overwhelmingly complex: There's a bunch of different "products", different models, acronyms to learn. How do you manage cost? How do you get the AI to do what you want, *how* you want it to do it.

I know myself well enough to know that I struggle with perfectionism. I feel like I need to know everything before I can get started. That, of course, is a problem for learning how to deal with AI, because the landscape is continuously changing. So my plan is to simply *jump in*.

My completely arbitrary tool to use: Claude Code.

My plan: start with some learning, the old fashioned way. I'm going to start by reading some documentation, and seeing what that inspires. I know I don't need to have a plan yet, I just need to get far enough along that I can answer the question: "What's next?"
